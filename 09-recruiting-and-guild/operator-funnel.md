# Operator funnel — the customer→operator flywheel

The conversion path that turns a Statement recipient into a dues-paying operator. It is
deliberately the *same shape* as the customer funnel (02→05), because the inputs are
already warm — these are people who already trust the stack.

---

## The path

```
CUSTOMER receives Statement (06)
   │  "Connect in the Alliance" hand-raise  ─►  operator_interest = true (08)
   ▼
APPLICANT  ─► /become-an-operator (01) or the operator-nurture email (02)
   │  Setmore books an interview (same tool as the consult, 03)
   ▼
VETTING  ─► background check · bond · references · agreement  (vetting-checklist.md)
   │  status: applicant → vetting → active
   ▼
ONBOARDED OPERATOR
   │  10: W-9 collected, contractor agreement e-signed (BEFORE first payout)
   │  09: assigned a route (02), set up dues (platform subscription)
   ▼
PRODUCES STATEMENTS for a book of homes  ─► back to 06 at fleet scale (the portfolio)
```

## Why it converts

- **Warm, not cold:** the applicant already lives with the product and got value from it.
- **The portfolio is the pitch:** show the operator Alliance Member Portfolio
  (`localDNS/docs/statements/operator/`) — "this is your dashboard; this is you earning
  your keep." The artifact sells the role.
- **Gig income, low ceremony:** dual-hat means they keep their own home's protection and
  add a side business; the generator + bootstrap make homes #2–#20 profitable instead of
  exhausting (the unit-economics story in `MARKETING`).

## Density coupling (with stage 02)

A new operator is matched to a **route**, not scattered homes — and a route that hits
target density without an operator is itself a recruiting trigger. The funnel and the
geo-targeting loop feed each other: density makes an operator viable; an operator makes
density serviceable.

## Open decisions (carried from MARKETING)

- Member **dues amount** + what they unlock.
- What "guild-certified" means concretely (the vetting standard — see
  [`vetting-checklist.md`](vetting-checklist.md)).
- Conversion-rate target (% of customers who become operators) to watch as the flywheel
  metric.
