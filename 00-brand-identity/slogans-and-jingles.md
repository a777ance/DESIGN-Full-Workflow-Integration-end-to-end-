# Slogans, jingle & the intro video

The approved taglines and the creative briefs for the audio and video. Everything here has
to pass the voice test in [`brand-kit.md`](brand-kit.md): calm, specific, honest, plain —
*pest control, not lawn care.*

---

## Taglines

**Lead with this one** (it owns the analogy, and the whole category-education play in
[`../02-demand-generation/category-education.md`](../02-demand-generation/category-education.md)
leans on it):

> ## "Pest control for your home internet."

Strong runners-up, for headlines and ad variations:

- **"The quiet is the product."**
- **"We make the invisible visible — every month."**
- **"A real person watching your back online."** — leads with the human, not the tech.
- **"You'll never see it working. That's the point."**

> The winner becomes the Google listing tagline (01) and the headline on the homepage (01).
> **Avoid anything fear-forward** ("hackers are coming for you!") — it breaks the calm voice
> and over-promises. We are the bouncer who makes the night boring, not the guy yelling
> about a break-in.

## Jingle brief

- **Length:** a 5–8 second sting, plus an optional 15-second bed for video.
- **Feel:** warm and domestic — the sound of a settled house, not a tech "whoosh." Think
  the exhale when you realize something you were worried about is handled.
- **Hook:** lands on the name or the category line; a homeowner should be able to hum it.
- **Deliverables:** the sting, a 15s bed, a 30s bed; WAV masters + MP3s. Linked from
  `brand-kit.md` once produced (`CHANGE_ME`).

## Intro-video brief

- **Length:** 60 seconds or less. It's a funnel asset — it lives on the homepage (01) and
  plays in the booking flow (03).
- **The story (same arc as the 2-minute pitch):**
  1. The invisible problem — the TV quietly phoning home, the tracker on the kid's tablet.
  2. The quiet someone keeps — the small box, the operator fixing things you never noticed.
  3. The proof — **show a real statement.** Scan the QR on screen and scroll it on a phone.
  4. The human — the named, local, vetted operator.
  5. One ask — book a free look (→ the intake form, 03).
- **Hard rule:** feature the **real** Statement from `localDNS/docs/statements/`, never a
  made-up dashboard. Show the scan-and-scroll.
- **Tone:** the warmth of a "Handled For You" line read out loud.
- **Deliverable:** MP4 (a 16:9 cut and a 9:16 cut for phones), captioned. Linked from
  `brand-kit.md` (`CHANGE_ME`).
