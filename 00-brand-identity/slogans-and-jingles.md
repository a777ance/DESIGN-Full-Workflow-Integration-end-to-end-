# Slogans, jingle & intro-video brief

Approved language and the creative briefs for the audio/video assets. All copy here must
pass the voice test in [`brand-kit.md`](brand-kit.md): calm, specific, honest, plain —
*pest control, not lawn care.*

---

## Slogans / taglines

Lead candidates (the category-education play in [`../02-demand-generation/`](../02-demand-generation/)
leans on the analogy):

- **"Pest control for your home network."** — the category line; owns the analogy.
- **"The quiet is the product."**
- **"Someone's watching, so the bad stuff stays out."**
- **"We make the invisible visible — every month."**
- **"A guild for your home network."** — leads with trust/people, not tech.

> Status: candidates, not locked. The winner becomes the GBP tagline (01) and the
> hero line on the site. Avoid fear-forward phrasing ("hackers are attacking you!") —
> it contradicts the calm voice and over-promises.

## Jingle brief

- **Length:** 5–8s sting + an optional 15s bed for video.
- **Feel:** warm, domestic, reassuring — a *settled* feeling, not a tech whoosh.
- **Mnemonic:** resolve on the category line or the name. Hummable by a homeowner.
- **Deliverables:** sting, 15s bed, 30s bed; WAV masters + MP3. Linked from
  `brand-kit.md` once produced (`CHANGE_ME`).

## Intro-video brief

- **Length:** ≤60s (a funnel asset for the site (01) and the demo step in (03)).
- **Arc:** (1) the invisible problem — the tracker, the unpatched TV; (2) the quiet
  someone keeps; (3) the monthly Statement as proof — *show the real artifact*; (4) the
  guild/human touch; (5) one CTA → the intake form.
- **Rule:** feature the **real** Statement from `localDNS/docs/statements/`, not a
  fictional dashboard. Show the QR-to-scroll experience.
- **Tone:** the named-operator warmth of the "Handled For You" log.
- **Deliverable:** MP4 (16:9 + a 9:16 cut for social), captioned. Linked from
  `brand-kit.md` (`CHANGE_ME`).
