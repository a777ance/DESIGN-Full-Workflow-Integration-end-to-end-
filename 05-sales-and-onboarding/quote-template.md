# The quote

A quote that's **specific to their house** — it names what they're getting, so the monthly
price is anchored to real value instead of a round number. Below is David Allum's, worked end
to end, then a blank you can copy. The prices are our working numbers (see `MARKETING`); the
setup fee is never discounted. When signed, it gets saved on the customer's record.

---

## Worked example — David Allum

> # A777ance — your quote
> **Prepared by:** Jose Marín · A777ance guild
> **For:** David Allum · 100-block, Maple Grove · (route RT-0001)
> **Date:** June 4, 2026
>
> ### What you get
> - A small managed box on your network that quietly keeps trackers, ads, and bad sites off
>   **every device** — your TV, your phone, the speaker — with no app to install.
> - Your internet company can no longer see the sites your house visits.
> - A **monthly statement** — plain-language proof of what we handled — by email (and on
>   paper if you want it), scrollable from a QR code.
> - A real person — me, Jose, two miles away — you can call.
>
> ### What we're covering for you
> | | Your home |
> | --- | --- |
> | Things on your Wi-Fi | ~12 |
> | What you care about most | Privacy (no provider snooping) + your TV not freezing mid-stream |
> | Mailed paper statement? | No — you'll use the phone version |
>
> ### The price
> | | Amount | |
> | --- | --- | --- |
> | One-time setup | **$175** | the visit + the install on your gear — never discounted |
> | Monthly | **$32** | the ongoing quiet + your statement |
>
> *No contract. Cancel anytime — the reason to stay is the monthly proof, not a lock-in.*
>
> ### Next step
> Sign below → we pick a day for the install → your first statement lands the following month.
