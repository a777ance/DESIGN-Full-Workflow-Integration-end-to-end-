**On QuickBooks specifically — the metamodern call, both poles named:**
- **Conservative pole:** QuickBooks Online is the industry standard. It's what a CPA expects, what a future LLC/audit wants, and it has clean Stripe sync. Best practice says set it up before revenue. It guards against the failure mode of losing deductible expenses.
- **Lean pole:** At $0 revenue and 2 cash-poor founders, $35/mo QuickBooks is a >100% increase to your entire burn to track *one customer*. **Wave** does double-entry books, P&L, and bank import for **free**, and exports clean if you migrate later. A spreadsheet ledger with my chart of accounts costs $0 and is honestly fine through customer #5.
- **My recommendation:** **Don't connect QuickBooks yet.** Start with Wave (free) *now* so the ledger exists from dollar one, and migrate to QuickBooks when you hit ~5 customers or form the LLC — whichever comes first. You get the "GL before revenue" discipline of FIN-002 without breaking the burn target. **CEO decides which pole.** I'll draft a superseding FIN-002b if you want the Wave path on record.
