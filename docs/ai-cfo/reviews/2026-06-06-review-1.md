## 4. CEO overnight brief — both questions answered

### Q1: Phase 1 lead-gen budget (door-to-door, one zip code)

**Model (all figures hypothesis — labeled):**

| Input | Assumption | Source |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| Flyers/cards | 500 units, local print shop | ~$30–60 |
| Door-to-door conversion | 0.5–2% (home-services range) | industry typical |
| Customers from 500 doors | 2.5 (low) to 10 (high) | 500 × rate |

**Implied CAC:** $60 ÷ 2.5 = **~$24/customer (bear)**; $30 ÷ 10 = **~$3/customer (bull)**. Call it **$3–24 CAC**.

**Payback against LTV:**
- LTV: $523 founding / $595 standard (setup + 12mo at zero churn).
- The **$175 setup fee alone covers CAC ~7–58× over on day one.** Payback is *immediate* — the setup fee clears CAC before the first monthly even runs.
- This is the rare channel where CAC is recovered at the point of sale. **A door-to-door flyer campaign at $30–60 is the single best-justified spend in the whole plan.**

**Recommendation:** ✅ One zip-code flyer run at **≤$60** fits inside one month's burn and is approved-shaped *pending CEO sign-off on the spend*. ✅ Deferring paid ads (Google/Meta) until 3 renewals validate pricing is **correct** — paid ads have no setup-fee-covers-CAC dynamic at our volume and would burn capital chasing an unvalidated price. **No channel in the Phase 1 plan requires upfront spend exceeding one month's burn.** Good plan; I agree with the CEO's hypothesis.

*One caveat: CAC math assumes Stripe is live to collect the setup fee. A flyer that drives a "yes" we can't bill is wasted spend — so sequence Stripe (#1) before the flyer drop.*

### Q2: CRM total cost of ownership

| Tool | Cost | Ceiling / friction point |
|------|------|--------------------------|
| **Google Sheets** | $0 | Breaks on *operational friction*, not cost — no pipeline view, no automated dunning reminders, no status triggers. Fine to ~10–15 customers if you're disciplined. The schema (Stage 08) already defines the record fields; a Sheet holds them. |
| **Airtable free** | $0 | 1,200 records / 5 editors. At 20 customers + 2 operators + routes ≈ **well under 100 records.** Free tier holds comfortably through all of Phase 1 and most of Phase 2. |
| **Airtable Plus** | ~$10/user/mo | Only needed past ~1,200 records (hundreds of customers) or when you want richer automation. **Not a Phase 1 or early Phase 2 cost.** |
| **HubSpot free** | $0 | Generous contact limits; paid (~$15/seat) only matters when you need sequences/reporting at scale. Heavier than needed now. |

**Bottom line — minimize cost with no painful migration:** Start on **Google Sheets ($0)** using the Stage 08 schema field names *exactly*. When friction bites (~Phase 2, pipeline view + reminders), **export CSV → import to Airtable free ($0).** Because the record matches the schema, that migration is a clean import, not a rebuild. **HubSpot is over-tooled for Phase 1–2** — skip it until contact volume genuinely demands sequences. **Projected CRM cost through Phase 2: $0.** This matches the CEO's hypothesis and I endorse it, with one tightening: define the schema-compliant columns *now* in the Sheet so the future Airtable migration is frictionless.
