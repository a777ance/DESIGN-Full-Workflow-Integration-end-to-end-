### Q1: Phase 1 lead-gen budget on limited capital

**Door-to-door single-zip campaign — modeled (all figures hypotheses):**

- **Materials:** ~500 flyers/door-hangers at a local shop ≈ **$30–60** one-time. Half-page cards run cheaper; door-hangers (no tape needed) convert better. Call it **$45 midpoint.**
- **Conversion (home services D2D benchmark):** 0.5–2%. On 500 doors → **2.5 to 10 customers** (bull/bear).
- **Implied CAC:** $45 ÷ 2.5 = **~$18/customer** (bear) to $45 ÷ 10 = **~$4.50/customer** (bull). Even adding the $175 setup fee *as revenue offset*, the setup fee alone covers CAC **~4–40×** over.
- **Payback vs. LTV ($595 std / $523 founding):** CAC is recovered **on the setup fee at signing** — payback is *immediate*, before month 1 of subscription. Even at the bear 0.5% rate, $18 CAC against a $523 founding LTV is a **~29× LTV:CAC ratio.** This is an exceptional channel *if the conversion holds* — and D2D for a trust-based local service with a physical appliance is a credible fit.

**Verdict on deferring paid ads:** **I agree — defer Google/Meta.** Paid ads before pricing is validated spends real cash to acquire customers at an *unvalidated* price and unproven Statement-delivery. You'd be buying customers you can't yet bill (B1) or deliver to (B2). D2D + free digital (Nextdoor, Google Business Profile) is the correct Phase 1 spend. Paid ads belong in Phase 2, after 3 renewals prove the price holds.

**Channels exceeding one month's burn (~$28):** Only the door-to-door materials at the **$60 high end** slightly exceeds one month's burn — and it's a **one-time** cost, not recurring, fully covered by the *first* setup fee. **Recommendation: cap the print run at ~$45 (≈500 units), CEO-approved as a one-time marketing expense.** This is a recommendation, not an authorization — **CEO signs off before the print order.** Everything else in Phase 1 is $0.

**The honest caveat:** the CAC math is dazzling *only if* a printed flyer can't be redeemed faster than B1/B2 clear. **A flyer that drives a "yes" you can't bill or deliver burns trust, not just paper.** Sequence it: Stripe live + Statement deploy confirmed *first*, then print. Don't knock doors for a product that can't transact.
