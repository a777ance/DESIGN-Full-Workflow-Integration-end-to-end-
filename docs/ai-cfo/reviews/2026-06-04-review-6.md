## 4. The 6-Year LLC Horizon (target: ~June 2032)

You asked to be in a healthy position to form an LLC in 6 years. Here's what I'd start banking *now* so that day is a formality, not a scramble:

1. **Clean books from transaction #1.** The single biggest gift to future-you is a continuous, categorized ledger. An LLC formed on top of 6 years of clean records is trivially financeable and auditable. An LLC formed on top of 6 years of shoebox receipts is a liability.
2. **Keep the two ledgers separate** (customer money in / operator money out) — your Stage 07 and Stage 10 docs already insist on this. Do it from day one; it's free.
3. **Don't comingle.** Right now you're two sole proprietors. The moment real money flows, open a **dedicated business bank account** (many are free — see §5) and run *everything* through it. Comingling personal and business funds is the #1 thing that pierces an LLC's liability shield later. Building the habit now costs nothing.
4. **Track the partnership split explicitly.** Two of you, cash-poor, sharing a business — decide *now*, in writing, how revenue and costs split. When you form the LLC (or an earlier partnership/multi-member LLC), this becomes your operating agreement. A handshake between friends is the most expensive document you'll never write.
5. **Why 6 years and not sooner?** An LLC costs $50–800/yr depending on state (CA is the painful one at $800/yr franchise tax). At <$30/mo burn and near-zero revenue, an LLC is pure cost with little benefit *today*. The trigger to form it earlier than 2032 is: **first operator you don't personally know, OR liability exposure on a customer's home network, OR revenue crossing ~$30–50k/yr.** Any of those three should pull the date forward. I'll watch for them.
