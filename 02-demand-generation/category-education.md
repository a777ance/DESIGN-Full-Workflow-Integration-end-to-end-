# Category education — owning the analogy

The market doesn't know it has a problem. Our job is to teach it — at scale, ownably,
and in the calm voice (00). We win by owning **one analogy** so completely that the
category and the brand become the same thought: *pest control for your home network.*

---

## The analogy, unpacked

| Pest control | A777ance |
| ------------ | -------- |
| Bugs you never see | Trackers, ad networks, unpatched IoT, cleartext DNS leaks |
| You pay monthly even when you see no pests | You pay monthly for the quiet — the absence of incidents |
| The sticker on the door proves someone came | The monthly **Statement** proves the work happened |
| A licensed, bonded technician you trust in your home | A vetted guild **operator** (09) |

The whole content program is this table, expanded. Every post maps one row.

## Content pillars (WordPress, stage 01)

1. **"Is your smart TV spying on you?"** — the invisible problem, made concrete.
2. **"Why grandma's tablet is a target."** — the elder-care / scam-protection angle
   (the strongest emotional version of "make the invisible visible").
3. **"What your ISP can see."** — cleartext DNS, in plain language.
4. **"What a month of protection actually looks like."** — walk through a real
   Statement from `localDNS/docs/statements/`.
5. **"Why a person, not an app."** — the guild/trust pitch; seeds the operator funnel (09).

Each post: plain language (a grandparent understands it), no fear-mongering, one CTA →
intake form (03).

## SEO posture

- **Local first:** the GBP (01) + location pages do the high-intent work.
- **Educational long-tail:** the pillars above own the "should I worry about X" queries.
- **Proof links:** posts link to the live gallery (`a777ance.github.io/localDNS/`).

## Guardrails

- Never claim a threat the data can't show (the kept-document honesty rule).
- Never lead with fear; lead with quiet. Fear converts once and churns; calm retains.
- Tie every piece back to the monthly Statement — the proof is the product.
